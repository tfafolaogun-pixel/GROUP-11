FIXIT (CAMPUS MAINTENANCE MANAGEMENT APPLICATION)
PRODUCT REQUIREMENT DOCUMENT(PRD)
1. Executive Summary
FixIt is a campus maintenance management application designed to streamline the process of reporting, assigning and resolving maintenance issues within university residence halls.
Currently, the maintenance reporting process in many student residences relies on physical logbooks and manual communication. Students experiencing problems such as faulty electrical installations, plumbing issues, damaged furniture and other room-related maintenance concerns are required to document their complaints and wait for the appropriate personnel to review and attend to them. This process can result in delayed responses, misplaced complaints, inefficient communication and a lack of accountability.
FixIt aims to address these challenges by providing a centralized digital platform that connects students, maintenance administrators and maintenance personnel. Through the application, students can submit maintenance requests, describe their problems, upload relevant images and monitor the progress of their complaints. Administrators can review requests, assign them to the appropriate maintenance personnel and monitor their progress, while maintenance workers can receive assigned jobs and update their resolution status.
The application will primarily serve university students living in residence halls, maintenance administrators and university maintenance personnel, including plumbers, electricians, carpenters and technicians.
The primary objective of FixIt is to improve the efficiency, transparency and accountability of campus maintenance operations by reducing dependence on manual reporting processes and providing a more organised approach to handling maintenance requests.
Ultimately, FixIt seeks to create a more convenient and reliable maintenance reporting experience for students while improving how universities manage and resolve accommodation-related maintenance issues.
2. Product Overview
FixIt is a digital campus maintenance management application designed to simplify and improve the process of reporting, managing and resolving maintenance issues within university residence halls.
The application provides a centralized platform that connects students, maintenance administrators and maintenance personnel, including plumbers, electricians, carpenters, technicians and other relevant professionals.
Through FixIt, students can conveniently report maintenance issues by selecting an appropriate category, providing a description of the problem, specifying their hostel and room location, and uploading relevant images where necessary. Once a request is submitted, students can track its progress and receive updates on its status.
Maintenance administrators can access submitted requests, review reported issues, prioritize them based on urgency, assign them to the appropriate maintenance personnel and monitor their progress. Maintenance personnel can view their assigned tasks, access relevant information about reported issues and update the status of their jobs as they progress towards resolution.
The application is intended to replace the traditional manual complaint-reporting process with a more organized, transparent and accessible digital system. By bringing students, administrators and maintenance personnel onto a single platform, FixIt aims to improve communication, promote accountability and make the management of maintenance requests more efficient within university residence halls.
3. Problem Statement
The current maintenance reporting process within university residence halls often relies on physical logbooks and manual communication. Students experiencing maintenance issues in their rooms are required to document their complaints and wait for the appropriate personnel to review and attend to them.
This process can create several challenges, including delayed responses, misplaced complaints, inefficient communication and a lack of transparency regarding the progress of reported issues.
Additionally, students often have limited visibility into whether their complaints have been received, assigned or attended to. Maintenance administrators may also find it difficult to efficiently organise, prioritise and monitor multiple maintenance requests through a manual reporting system.
The absence of a centralised and efficient maintenance management platform creates a need for a digital solution that simplifies complaint submission, improves communication between students and maintenance personnel, and provides a structured approach to tracking and resolving maintenance issues.
FixIt seeks to address these challenges by digitising the maintenance reporting and management process.
4. Target Audience (Stake holders and Users)
•	Students: Report maintenance issues and track their complaints.
•	Administrators: Review complaints, prioritise them and assign workers.
•	Maintenance Personnel: Receive assigned tasks, carry out repairs and update request statuses.

5. Proposed Solution
FixIt proposes a centralised digital platform that enables students to report and track maintenance issues while allowing administrators to review, prioritise and assign requests to the appropriate maintenance personnel. The application aims to replace manual reporting with a more organised and transparent system that improves communication, accountability and maintenance management within university residence halls.

7. Success Metrics
The success of FixIt will be measured by its ability to improve the efficiency, transparency and accountability of maintenance management within university residence halls. The following metrics will be used to evaluate the application's performance:
1.	Request Submission Rate: Measure the number of maintenance requests successfully submitted through the application.
2.	Average Response Time: Track the time taken for administrators or maintenance personnel to acknowledge submitted requests.
3.	Average Resolution Time: Measure the average time required to resolve reported maintenance issues.
4.	Request Resolution Rate: Determine the percentage of submitted maintenance requests successfully resolved.
5.	User Satisfaction: Evaluate student satisfaction with the reporting process and the handling of maintenance complaints.
6.	Pending Requests: Monitor the number of requests awaiting review, assignment or resolution.
7.	Request Reopening Rate: Track the percentage of resolved requests reopened due to unresolved or recurring issues.
8.	User Adoption Rate: Measure the percentage of students within the target residence halls actively using the application.
8.Functional Requirements
The functional requirements of FixIt define the essential features and capabilities the application must provide to support the reporting, management and resolution of maintenance issues within university residence halls.
The application shall provide the following functionalities:
1.	User Authentication: The system shall allow students, administrators and maintenance personnel to securely log in and access features based on their assigned roles.
2.	Maintenance Request Submission: The system shall allow students to submit maintenance complaints by selecting an issue category, providing a description, specifying their hostel and room location, and uploading relevant images.
3.	Request Management: The system shall allow administrators to view, review and prioritise submitted maintenance requests according to their urgency.
4.	Request Assignment: The system shall allow administrators to assign maintenance requests to the appropriate maintenance personnel based on the nature of the reported issue.
5.	Request Tracking: The system shall allow students to monitor the progress and current status of their submitted maintenance requests.
6.	Task Management: The system shall allow maintenance personnel to view their assigned tasks, access relevant complaint details and update the progress of their work.
7.	Status Updates: The system shall maintain and display the status of maintenance requests throughout the reporting and resolution process.
8.	Notifications: The system shall notify relevant users when important changes occur to their maintenance requests, including submission, assignment and resolution.

9.	User Stories
•	As a student, I want to report maintenance issues through the application so that I can conveniently submit complaints without relying on physical logbooks.
•	As a student, I want to track the status of my maintenance requests so that I can stay informed about the progress of reported issues.
•	As an administrator (Hall admins, porters e.t.c), I want to review, prioritize and assign submitted requests so that maintenance issues can be handled by the appropriate personnel.
•	As a  Technician (maintenance worker),, I want to view my assigned tasks and update their progress so that administrators and students can stay informed about the repair process.
•	As a student, I want to receive notifications about changes to my maintenance requests so that I know when my complaints have been reviewed, assigned or resolved.
10.	Non-Functional Requirements
The non-functional requirements of FixIt define the quality standards and conditions the application must meet to ensure a secure, reliable, accessible and efficient user experience.
1.	Performance: The application should load pages and process user requests efficiently, with normal pages loading within 3 seconds under expected operating conditions.
2.	Security: The application shall protect users' personal information and ensure that students, administrators and maintenance personnel can only access features and information permitted by their assigned roles.
3.	Usability: The application should have a simple, intuitive and user-friendly interface that allows users with different levels of technical experience to navigate and perform tasks easily.
4.	Reliability: The application should operate consistently and minimise system failures, data loss and interruptions during normal usage.
5.	Scalability: The application should support an increasing number of students, maintenance requests and users without significant degradation in performance.
6.	Availability: The application should be accessible whenever users need to submit, manage or track maintenance requests, subject to internet connectivity and scheduled maintenance.
7.	Compatibility: The application should function properly across commonly used web browsers and devices, including smartphones, tablets and computers.
8.	Data Integrity: The application shall maintain accurate and consistent maintenance records, ensuring that request details and status updates are properly stored and retrieved.
11. Project Scope
The scope of FixIt covers the development of a digital campus maintenance management application designed to improve how maintenance issues are reported, assigned, tracked and resolved within university residence halls.
The first version of the application (MVP) will focus on the essential functionalities required to connect students, maintenance administrators and maintenance personnel.
11.1 In-Scope Features
The following features will be included in the initial development of FixIt:
1.	User Registration and Authentication: Allow students, administrators and maintenance personnel to create accounts, log in and access features based on their assigned roles.
2.	Maintenance Request Submission: Allow students to report maintenance issues by selecting an appropriate category, providing a description, specifying their hostel and room location, and uploading relevant images.
3.	Maintenance Request Management: Allow administrators to view, review and prioritise submitted maintenance requests.
4.	Worker Assignment: Allow administrators to assign maintenance requests to the appropriate maintenance personnel based on the nature of the reported issue.
5.	Request Tracking: Allow students to monitor the progress and current status of their submitted maintenance requests.
6.	Worker Task Management: Allow maintenance personnel to view assigned tasks, access complaint details and update the progress of their work.
7.	Status Updates and Notifications: Allow the system to update request statuses and notify relevant users when important changes occur.
8.	Request History: Allow students and authorised administrators to access records of previously submitted maintenance requests.
9.	Student Feedback: Allow students to provide feedback on completed maintenance requests.
11.2 Out-of-Scope Features
The following features will not be included in the initial version of FixIt but may be considered for future development:
1.	AI-Powered Issue Detection: Automatically identifying or diagnosing maintenance problems from uploaded images.
2.	Automatic Worker Assignment: Automatically assigning maintenance requests to workers based on their skills, availability or location.
3.	Predictive Maintenance: Using historical maintenance data to predict potential faults before they occur.
4.	Advanced Analytics and Reporting: Providing detailed performance reports, maintenance trends and analytical dashboards.
5.	Worker Scheduling: Managing workers' shifts, availability and work schedules within the application.
6.	Inventory and Spare Parts Management: Tracking maintenance materials, tools and spare parts used during repairs.
7.	Payment Processing: Handling payments for maintenance services through the application.
8.	Multi-University Support: Expanding the platform to manage maintenance operations across multiple universities.
11.3 Scope Limitations
FixIt will initially focus on maintenance management within university residence halls. The application will facilitate the reporting, assignment and tracking of maintenance issues but will not directly carry out physical repair activities or guarantee immediate resolution of reported problems.
